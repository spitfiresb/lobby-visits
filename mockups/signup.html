How this connects

  • Real page: static/signup.html, served at /signup.
  • Submit sends POST /auth/signup with {"email", "password"}.
    • 201: the account is created and you're signed in (cookie set), so the page goes to /visits.
    • 403: not on the allowlist (message above).
    • 409: already has an account. 400: missing email or short password.
  • The email is trimmed and lowercased on the server, so " Marcus.Vitale@Brightline.example " still matches.
